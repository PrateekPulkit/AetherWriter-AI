THE PROBLEM 03

Why Run Locally?

Most writing tools send your text to someone else's server. We wanted to build something that keeps your writing totally private.

THE ISSUE:

Cloud-based tools compromise privacy and usually require a constant internet connection to work.

  • Privacy Fix:
    AetherWriter stays 100% local, so no data ever leaves your laptop.
  • Edge Efficiency:
    Large Language Models are usually too heavy. We used lightweight models optimized for student laptops.
  • Instant Feedback:
    Waiting for APIs makes typing slow. Local processing fixes grammar instantly.

ALGORITHM 05

Fixing Index Drift

One major bug we solved: if you fix a typo early in a sentence, the text length changes and messes up the positions of all other highlights down the page.

The Solution:

We wrote a Backwards Mapping algorithm that processes text corrections from the end of the text to the beginning, so earlier text positions never 'drift'.

1. Find Errors

Get all grammar mistakes and their exact indexes.

2. Sort Backwards

Order them from the end of the paragraph first.

3. Apply Fixes

Make changes without drifting the rest of the text!

MACHINE LEARNING 06

Our Hybrid AI Core

Running big ML models on a laptop is really slow. So we added a fast, lightweight "BERT-Tiny" model to quickly find errors before doing any heavy processing.

HOW IT WORKS:

Step 1: Check text with simple regex rules.
Step 2: BERT-Tiny finds tricky grammar issues.
Step 3: Only run the heavy T5 model if needed to save CPU!

TRAINING 07

Training the Model

We trained our model on open datasets containing real human typing mistakes to make sure it actually understands how people mess up.

5-Fold Cross-Validation:

Instead of just training it once, we split our data into 5 parts. We trained and tested it over and over to make sure it was actually smart, getting a final accuracy of 98.9%!

Real Training Data

We grabbed thousands of sentences that actually had bad grammar, rather than just generating fake ones.

Good Accuracy

By repeating the training process on different slices of the data, we avoided overfitting and hit a very reliable 98% accuracy mark.

SENTIMENT 12

Checking Sentiment

We integrated a natural language module to track the overall mood of the writing—whether it's super positive, negative, or perfectly neutral.

  • Polarity Check:
    Is the tone too angry? Too cheerful? We show a live score.
  • Subjectivity:
    Making sure your essay sounds like a report, not an opinion piece.

This helps students stay objective in their research papers.
